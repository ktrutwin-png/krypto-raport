This is the actual system that runs @erl.grey83 – built by one person, not a team. Below: every part, what it really does, and what it does not do. No hype, no screenshots of fake numbers.

🧠 The Brain – Claude (Anthropic). Plans the work, writes the rules for every agent, checks their results and fixes what breaks. Agents don't talk to the owner during the week – they report to the Brain, and the owner gets one short weekly report.

How one video gets made

  1. The agent reads its plan and the lessons from past mistakes.
  2. Pulls live prices and checks today's news against at least two sources.
  3. Writes a short script – facts only, one question left to the viewer.
  4. Generates visuals, renders 1–2 real 3D scenes, adds voice and captions.
  5. Watches its own frames (start, middle, end) and re-renders anything unreadable.
  6. Publishes, then logs the result so next week's numbers can be compared.

What this system does NOT do

  • It does not trade real money. Every bot is on a demo, paper or dry-run account.
  • It does not sell signals, courses or "guaranteed" strategies.
  • It does not reply to DMs automatically – a person reads them.
  • It does not promise profit. Markets can and do go against any system.
