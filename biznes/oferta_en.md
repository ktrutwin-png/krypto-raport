# Offer – AI automation for small businesses (EN)
Version 1 · 2026-11-09 · prices excl. VAT · DRAFT – prices approved by the owner before sending

Simple, working automations for salons, restaurants, garages, clinics and shops. No long contracts – monthly, 30 days' notice.

## Package 1 – "Customer Bot" (WhatsApp / Telegram)
- 24/7 answers to common questions (hours, prices, directions, services) – FAQ built from your material (up to 40 questions).
- Booking request: the bot collects name, service and preferred time and notifies you (or links your booking system).
- Hand-over to a human with one word ("staff"); 2 languages; short guide + 30 min online walkthrough.
**Delivery:** 7–10 working days · **Price:** one-off **€595** · care **€59/month**. Platform fees (WhatsApp Business API conversation fees by Meta) per provider; Telegram free.

## Package 2 – "Social media on autopilot"
- 8 short vertical videos per month (Reels/Shorts/TikTok), 20–40 s, synthetic voice-over, captions, music.
- Template in your brand colours, auto-scheduling (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube Shorts), captions + hashtags, monthly report.
- AI images always labelled as AI.
**Delivery:** 10 working days · **Price:** one-off **€295** · **€349/month**.

## Package 3 – "AI assistant for your business"
- Draft replies to recurring e-mails, ready in your inbox – you press "send".
- Automatic mail sorting (customer / invoice / spam / urgent); document templates (quote, confirmation, reminder).
- The assistant never sends anything by itself.
**Delivery:** 10–15 working days · **Price:** one-off **€950** · care **€99/month**.

30% off the one-off fee in exchange for permission to describe the result (anonymous or named). Real numbers after 30 days only.
