JPMorgan, long a Bitcoin skeptic, now expects Bitcoin to beat stocks and gold over 10 years. Should you believe it?

- JPMorgan's 2027 long-term outlook (released Oct 19) pencils in 10% a year for Bitcoin, vs 7.5% for US large-cap stocks and 5.5% for gold
- Bitcoin's expected volatility is 55% a year, about 3x gold's; that swing drags a ~21% average return down to a 10% compound return
- Bitcoin is not included as an allocation in JPMorgan's model portfolios, and this is a long-term estimate, not a trading signal
