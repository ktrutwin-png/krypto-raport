$6.4B stolen from Binance — the biggest crypto theft on record? 🚨

Here is what we know (as of Oct 30):
• Funds left 10 blockchains in about 20 minutes. TRM Labs says the attacker used an Ethereum feature (EIP-7702) and a wallet funded via Tornado Cash.
• About $2.7B in stablecoins was frozen, but TRM says 420K+ ETH and 18,270 BTC (~$2.4B) remain out of issuers' reach.
• Binance says withdrawals should resume within 48 hours and has set aside $6.34B of its own funds. TRM and Chainalysis point to North Korea's TraderTraitor group; North Korea has denied hacking allegations.
