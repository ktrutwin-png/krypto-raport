Japan bank outage: 128 banks went offline at once — ATMs, counters and online banking frozen 🏦⚠️

• Nov 6, ~1:30 pm: 128 shinkin banks in western Japan, about 3,430 branches, could not process transactions
• Cause, per Shinkin Central Bank: suspected hardware fault at one shared data center — no signs of a cyberattack
• Not a collapse: the banks are not insolvent, the money is there — people just couldn't reach it
• Not the first time: a 2-day payment outage in Oct 2023, 140 credit co-ops down in Jan 2026
