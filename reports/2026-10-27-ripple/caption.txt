🚨 Ripple is now a U.S. National Trust Bank.

The OCC gave its FINAL approval on Oct 26, 2026 for Ripple National Trust Bank:
🏦 It will manage and act as trustee for the reserves behind RLUSD (~$2.5B stablecoin)
🔐 It will offer digital-asset custody for institutions
🇺🇸 Road: applied July 2025 → conditional approval Dec 2025 → final approval now

"Crypto and banks work better together." — Brad Garlinghouse, Ripple CEO

Good to know: this is a trust bank, not a regular bank — no word on deposits or loans.
