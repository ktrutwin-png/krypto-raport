Bitcoin dipped to $75.7K on US election night — then bounced 3.6% by morning 🗳️

What happened (as of Nov 4):
• Democrats won control of both the House and the Senate. Prediction markets had largely expected it, and BTC slid about 3% from its pre-poll level to $75,736 before recovering above $78,000.
• Analysts point to risk cut before the vote, then buying back once the result was clear, helped by a weaker US dollar. Talos says the move was close to what options had priced in.
• US spot Bitcoin ETFs saw about $1.16B of net outflows over three sessions (SoSoValue), though Tuesday's $116M was the smallest. Some analysts expect slower, tougher crypto rules, and the market structure bill may slip to 2027.
